Thank you for reviewing Lightly. Here is the information you asked for. A screen recording from an iPhone is attached.

**1. Screen recording**
The attached recording was made on an iPhone running the latest iOS. It starts at app launch and shows the typical flow: choosing a photo, applying a preset and Auto, the editing tools, and saving a copy to Photos. Lightly has no account registration, login or account deletion, no user-generated content shared with other people, and no paid content or in-app purchases, so there is nothing of that kind to show.

**2. Purpose and audience**
Lightly is a photo editor for anyone who wants their photos to look the way they remember the moment, without learning professional editing software. You open a photo, apply a one-tap automatic correction or a curated preset, and fine-tune it with simple tools: background blur and replacement, portrait retouching, cropping and adjustments, effects, text, a watermark and a border. Lightly saves the result as a new copy, and the original photo is never changed.

**3. How to use the main features**
No login, credentials or sample files are needed. Any photo works.
- Launch the app and tap "Choose a photo" (the system photo picker; no library permission is needed) or "Camera".
- Develop: tap the wand for automatic correction, or tap a preset in the gallery below the photo.
- The dock along the bottom opens the other tools: Background, Portrait (shown when the photo contains a person; use a photo with a visible face), Edit (crop, rotate, adjust, remove), Effects, Watermark, Text and Border.
- Tap Save to write a copy to Photos. iOS asks for add-only Photos access the first time.
- More (⋮) has Appearance, Favourite presets, Saved signature, About and Legal.

**4. External services**
None. Lightly works fully offline and makes no network requests. There are no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, authentication or payment services. All processing runs on the device: Apple frameworks (Core Image, Vision, Metal, Core ML) and image models bundled inside the app. No data or photos leave the device.

**5. Regional differences**
None. The app works the same in every region where it is offered.

**6. Regulated industry or third-party material**
Lightly does not operate in a regulated industry. The bundled presets, fonts and sample assets are licensed for use in the app.
