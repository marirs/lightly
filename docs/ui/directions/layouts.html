Lightly — revised minimal design across six layouts

Develop, Portrait and Background are shown on each layout, in light and dark. The layouts are designed for each size, not scaled phone screens. The tool dock becomes a vertical rail when the controls sit beside the photo. The photo goes wherever it gets the most room. On the unfolded foldable, nothing crosses the hinge. On larger screens, categories become a full list. Every Develop ruler is live and loads all nine categories from the fixed catalogue.

Simulated: photo appearance (preset looks, Auto, skin, blur) is an illustration made with plain filters and an elliptical mask. Lightly's real rendering is not shown or validated. Each frame is drawn at its real point/dp size and reduced here only to fit the board.
