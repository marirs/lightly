Lightly — two visual directions

Each direction uses the same photos and the same four screens: Welcome, the Develop editor on a landscape, the contextual Portrait editor, and Background › Focus & Blur. The directions differ in layout and in how you move between tools and presets, not only in colour. The preset sliders are live: they load all 2,591 presets in the fixed nine-category catalogue, with every stop in its fixed order.

Simulated: photo appearance in these mockups (preset looks, Auto, blur, skin) is an illustration made with simple filters. Lightly's real rendering is not shown and is not validated.
Direction A

Darkroom — full-bleed photo, tool dock, detent ruler

  • Photo edge to edge. Controls float in dark glass over the photo, so it keeps the full screen height.
  • One persistent tool dock. Seven labelled tools in a scrolling dock at the bottom, about 4½ visible. The active tool's controls open in a panel just above it.
  • Presets on a detent ruler. Every stop is a tick, 12 pt apart. You scroll the ruler under a fixed needle; it previews while moving and commits when it settles. Long categories are a fast fling plus a precise snap.
  • Sub-tools as tabs. Inside a tool, options are text tabs (Skin · Under-eye · …), each showing only its own sliders.
Direction B

Gallery — framed print, mode carousel, precision scrubber

  • Photo as a print. An inset, rounded photo on warm paper, with Undo, Redo and Compare placed on the photo itself. A calm, editorial feel.
  • Tools as a camera-style mode carousel. Swipe the tool names at the bottom, as in the Camera app's modes. There is no row of buttons, and one tool fills the panel at a time.
  • Presets on a precision scrubber. The full category is drawn as one band of stops. Drag sideways to move quickly; slide your finger up while dragging for fine, one-stop control. A loupe names the neighbouring presets.
  • Sub-tools as an accordion. Every option shows its current value at a glance; one row opens at a time. Faces appear as round crops of the photo.
