# Reset controls — approved 9 October 2026

The owner approved the photo-overlay proposal: retain the local Reset action and put Reset all beside the magic wand at the bottom-left of the photo. Reset all appears only while edits exist, including Auto. It uses an opaque theme-dependent pill, blue label, border and separate touch area so bright and dark photos cannot hide it.

Local Reset offers the current adjustment and its whole section. Portrait adjustment reset applies to the selected face. Reset all confirms with “Reset all edits?” and “Return to the original photo. This also turns Auto off. You can undo this.” Actions: “Reset all edits” and “Keep editing”. A reset is one Undo step; saved signatures and favourites remain available.

Approval reference: lightly-photo-reset.html in the conversation visualization folder. Existing design reference images are unchanged. Category browsing and lifecycle behaviour are outside this change.
