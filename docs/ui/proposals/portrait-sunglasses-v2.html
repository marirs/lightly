1 · Add a pair

Ten pairs, shown as their own thumbnails in a scrolling row after None. The chosen pair lands on the eyes as a 3D frame: sized, tilted and turned with the head, arms running back to the ears and tucked under hair. Lenses: Dark, Brown, Green, Gradient, Mirror; the eyes stay visible through them. Darkness 0–100. Size fine-tunes the automatic fit (−50 to +50). None removes them.
