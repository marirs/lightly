Jaybird is a bold vintage signature script with the easy confidence of a hand-painted sign.

The connected, monoline-weight lettering leans forward with looping ascenders and quick tapered terminals — the kind of script brushed across a mid-century soda crate, a diner window, or the headstock of an old guitar. Each letter is drawn with even pressure and a generous, rounded rhythm, so words read warm and immediate rather than fussy. The capitals swing in with a big entry stroke and the lowercase joins cleanly, keeping a smooth, legible flow at any size.

It feels at home on a bottle label, a record sleeve, a motorcycle tank, or any mark that wants to look established and a little bit golden.

The font is built with advanced OpenType functionality and guaranteed top-notch quality, containing stylistic and contextual alternates, ligatures and more automatic and manual features; all to give you full control and customizability.
It has extensive lingual support, covering all Latin-based languages, from North Europa to South Africa, from America to South-East Asia. It contains all characters and symbols you'll ever need, including all punctuation and numbers.
